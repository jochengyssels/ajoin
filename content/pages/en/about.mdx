# Our story

In Aalst, people have been called the *Ajuinen*, the onions, for as long as anyone can remember. The nickname stuck so well that the town wears it with pride: onions on the carnival floats, onions on the merchandise, onions raining down on the Grote Markt during the carnival ajuinworp.

Ajoin & Shine starts from that same pride. We took the most Aalst ingredient there is and put it where you'd least expect it: in a shampoo.

## Carnival, every day

Aalst carnival is three days a year of colour, music and not taking yourself too seriously. We wanted a shampoo that feels like that. The bottle is loud and the mascot wears a jester's cap. The shampoo itself is made for every ordinary morning in between.

## What's in the bottle

Our shampoo is built around onion extract. You'll find the full ingredient list, in INCI format, on the product page, so you can see exactly what you're putting on your hair.
