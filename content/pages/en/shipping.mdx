# Shipping & returns

## Shipping

We ship to Belgium, the Netherlands, France and Italy. Rates include VAT.

<ShippingTable locale={props.locale} />

Delivery times are in working days from dispatch. You'll get an email with tracking once your parcel is on its way.

## Returns

You can withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of delivery, without giving a reason. Use the [online withdrawal function](./withdrawal) and we'll email you return instructions.

For hygiene reasons, opened products can't be returned. Unopened, sealed products can.

We refund the purchase price and the standard shipping cost within 14 days of your withdrawal, once we've received the goods back or you've shown proof you sent them.

## Damaged or wrong item?

Contact us at once through the [contact form](./contact) with your order number and a photo. We'll replace it or refund you.
