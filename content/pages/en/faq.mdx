# Frequently asked questions

## Orders and shipping

### Where do you ship?
To Belgium, the Netherlands, France and Italy. You choose the country in your cart and see the shipping cost before you pay.

### How long does delivery take?
See [Shipping & returns](./shipping) for delivery times per country.

### How do I find my order?
Go to [Find your order](./orders) and enter your email address and order number. We'll email you a link to it.

## Returns

### Can I return my order?
You can withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of delivery. Use the [online withdrawal form](./withdrawal). For hygiene reasons, a bottle whose seal has been broken can't be returned. See the [terms](./terms) for details.
