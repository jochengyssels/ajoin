Ask anyone in Aalst what they're called and you'll get the same answer: *Ajuinen*, onions. The nickname is old enough that nobody is quite sure where it started, and loved enough that the town turned it into a mascot.

At carnival you see it everywhere: on floats, on hats, on the crowd. And there's the *ajuinworp*, when onions are thrown from above onto the packed Grote Markt and people scramble to catch one.

We liked the idea of a nickname you wear with pride. So we named our hair care after it, gave the onion a jester's cap and sunglasses, and put it in the shower where you'll see it every morning.

Ajoin & Shine: the onion, but make it shine.
